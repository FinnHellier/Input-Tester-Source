Fighting Game Input Tester

Do I need to be good at fighting games?
No, inexperienced players are useful samples due to the lack of gameplay biases
Prompts have also been added to make it easier to understand
If the challenges become too difficult, you may quit at any time. Partial data is usable, but full completion is appreciated.
What data is collected?
Randomly assigned ID
Your previous related gaming experience
Your input device
Your performance in the test (Attempts taken and failure points)
All data will be stored either locally or in AWS servers both will comply with GDPR.

Can I retry afterwards?
Yes, additional attempts will be recorded and may also be analysed.


Can I have my data deleted?
Yes, before the deadline of April 15th.

What is the point of doing this?
The data will look into trends on:
Which buttons are easier to press
Which directional inputs are easier to perform
How input devices affect performance
This information may inform discussions in game balance, controller legality and groundwork for further research
