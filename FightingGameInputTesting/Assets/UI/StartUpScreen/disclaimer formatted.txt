<size=150%><b>Fighting Game Input Tester</b></size>

<b>Do I need to be good at fighting games?</b>
No. Inexperienced players are useful samples due to the lack of gameplay biases.
Prompts have also been added to make failures easier to understand.
If the challenges become too difficult, you may quit at any time.
Partial data is usable, but full completion is appreciated.

<b>What data is collected?</b>
- Randomly assigned ID
- Your previous related gaming experience
- Your input device
- Your performance in the test (attempts taken and failure points)
All data will be stored either locally or on AWS servers. Both comply with GDPR.

<b>Can I retry afterwards?</b>
Yes. Additional attempts will be recorded and may also be analysed.

<b>Can I have my data deleted?</b>
Yes, before the deadline of <b>April 15th</b>.

<b>What is the point of doing this?</b>
The data will look into trends such as:
- Which buttons are easier to press  
- Which directional inputs are easier to perform  
- How input devices affect performance  
This information may inform discussions about game balance,
controller legality, and may help lay groundwork for further research.
