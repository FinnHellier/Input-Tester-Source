<size=150%><b>Fighting Game Input Tester</b></size>

This is a study to record performance of players completing mechanical input challenges in the setting of a fighting game. 
This will be looking into all levels of play, so new and experienced players are both invited to participate.

Overall the goal of this is to have a dataset to analyse which can identify specific trends like certain buttons being more difficult to press, repeated inputs and motions in inputs.
This could then be used as a tool to balance fighting games as well as discussing if input devices give an advantage in practice.

To participate open this link: https://finnhellier.github.io/FightingGameInputTester/ and enter your experience and input device after agreeing to participate. 
The time commitment should be from around 5-20 minutes, if you get stuck and don't believe you can progress any further you may close the website and your results will be recorded. 

To delete your data send the ID given when entering your experience to this email: finnhthrowaway@gmail.com before the 15th of April, after this point data will be analysed and cannot be removed from the set.

Do not participate if you have any pre existing medical conditions that could have negative effects from performing tasks such as gaming, typing or writing. 
Do not continue in the study if you feel any discomfort.

All data will be stored anonymously.
Data will be stored following GDPRs guidelines, all data will be stored in an AWS RDS server and will be downloaded and processed locally on the deadline for data deletion. 
Experience, input device, randomly assigned ID, sequence seed and failure points during test will be recorded.
